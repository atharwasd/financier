Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Financier is a personal app. It has no servers and collects no data for its developer.

What the app stores

Your accounts, transactions, budgets and settings are stored on your own devices.

Google account data

If you sign in with Google, Financier requests only:

  • drive.appdata: to read and write one file, financier-data.json, in a hidden app-specific folder in your own Google Drive. The app cannot see any other files in your Drive.
  • Your email address: shown in the app so you know which account is signed in.

This data is used only to sync your records between your own devices. It is never sent to the developer or any third party, never sold, and never used for advertising.

Receipt and statement scanning

Images and documents you scan are processed by an AI model (Ollama) running on your own computer. They are not uploaded to any cloud service.

Deleting your data

Sign out in Settings and uninstall the app. To remove the synced copy, go to Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Financier → Delete hidden app data. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
